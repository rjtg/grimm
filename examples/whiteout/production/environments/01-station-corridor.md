# Aurora envelope and `loc.aurora_corridor`

## Canonical function [C]

Aurora is a polar research station, losing power at 03:17. Snow erases the
horizon through windows; lights fail from laboratory towards sleeping quarters.
Four crew members are together, Jonas's ankle is crushed, radio repeats a call,
intake is freezing and antenna motors are dark. The corridor establishes a
shared world and leads into stores, not a new exploration puzzle. Source:
`blackout.arrival`, screenplay B01; later `ending_lost.arrival` returns to Aurora.

## Architecture / accesses [A]

Use the same low modular exterior silhouette in the first distant view and lost
ending. Radio/mast belongs on the west/left side of the shared plan, engineering/
exhaust on east/right; this is a staging orientation, not geographical canon.
No added control tower, landing pad, underground bunker or second rescue route.

The corridor is a proposed 2.4m-wide shared transfer axis with stores' single
threshold off it and dispatch beyond. Laboratory/sleeping directions can be
shown by wall continuity and the dying light strip; no accessible new room is
built or offered there. The first and last light failure directions remain the
same in reverse coverage. High sealed windows admit cool snow bounce, not an
open escape. PLANS distinguishes physical adjacency from authored traversals.

## Object placement / cast / blocking [A]

Four-face group has Jonas low against a safe wall, Mara beside him, Alex with
clear access to his other side, Lea holding radio clear of his leg. A floor-level
injury insert returns to that exact group. Proposed unobstructed transfer lane
at least 2m where a partnered carry is staged; doorway clear width target 2m.
These are layout checks, not a claim of finished rig or medical validation.

`prop.torch`, `prop.handset`, `prop.splint` (only later after treatment), dark
antenna indicators and intake view. The repeated call is sound/handset acting;
no unexplained ghost projection or new figure at the window. The architecture
must not visually accuse Jonas before optional knowledge is acquired.

## Light / weather / cameras [A]

Ceiling strip extinguishes sequentially; torch plus snow fill keeps all four
legible. Limit frost inside to glass/frame edges, threshold moisture and the
failing intake; no whiteout particle storm through intact corridor walls.
Use proposed `cam.corridor_establish` then existing `cam.radio_insert`,
`cam.injury_insert`. Keep the group axis on one side; re-establish before crossing.
A masked/over-shoulder Alex view preserves open player casting from the Character
Bible. Dying lights, torch sweep and radio close-up overlap within the canonical
12000ms opening rather than adding a new delay or timer.

## Variants / source protection

**[C]** Opening power outage; cast commits to stores only after `blackout.begin`.
**[A]** Layout concept has no people so it cannot imply a completed carry.
No early log content, treatment or free inventory is shown as acquired.
**[O]** Exterior exact dimensions, corridor sectional height/window count,
safe carry contact and final mask/face framing need layout/casting review.
The exterior concept is paired with dispatch in the selected station sheet;
it is not a separate playable exterior scene.
