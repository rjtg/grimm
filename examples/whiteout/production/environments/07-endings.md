# Terminal locations — match the count, not a new escape map

These are the remaining three inherited location IDs. No new playable access,
room, time budget or post-ending traversal is introduced. Character/survivor,
vehicle, log and prop variants follow existing outcomes, not a new scene FSM.

## `loc.rescue_cabin` — `ending_full` / `ending_partial`

**[C] Function:** full rescue has four named answers in a helicopter; partial
has a missing person, helicopter following call or coastal patrol following
fallback. Injured Jonas remains injured if alive; tag/handset codas name actual
loss. Source `ending_full.arrival`, `ending_partial.*`, screenplay E01/E03/E04.

**[A] Space/access:** a compact transport tableau, four fixed seating/support
positions and manifest line of sight. Practical entry is an outgoing transfer
image, not another selectable door. Helicopter or coastal-patrol dressing is
chosen according to real call/fallback; do not invent a helicopter where no
bearing exists or a new named pilot. Coastal vehicle form remains [O]. No
full rescue simulation, helipad or navigable cockpit is required.

**[A] Objects/cast:** `prop.rescue_vehicle`, `prop.manifest`, actual splint/tag/
handset variants. Low frame retains protected ankle/boot clearance, Mara's
count and each real survivor. Empty seat must correspond to Jonas or Lea,
not a random background passenger. Four-position order is fixed across full/
partial match shots; do not repopulate a casualty to improve composition.

**[A] Light/weather/shots:** softer vehicle practical against storm seen outside,
not magically changed cast/clean clothes. `cam.manifest_four` and
`cam.manifest_empty` share seating axis; `cam.tag_insert` only Jonas-loss,
`cam.empty_handset` Lea-loss. Rotor or patrol engine sound follows chosen vehicle.
**[O]** Final seat dimensions, carried-body transfer, pickup geography and short
caption/VO timing need production rehearsal. Existing 1800ms cards are not a
promise that long dialogue fits.

## `loc.daylight_report` — optional truth codas

**[C] Function:** `log_read` permits explanation clearing Jonas: failed test-call
relay and de-icer overload, not sabotage. Truth cannot resurrect a lost person.
Source `ending_full.truth`, `ending_partial.truth`, screenplay E02/E04.

**[A] Space/access:** tabletop/document vignette in daylight, not an explorable
investigation office. No new investigator cast, door or hidden route. Reuse
`prop.maintenance_log` sketch, report/manifest context; props keep the same
signature and wiring marks. Missing log means no explanatory insert, not a
free revelation from an environment camera.

**[A] Light/weather/shots:** `cam.report_insert` on the same recovered paper under
soft daylight. In partial, preserve empty-place grief in adjoining composition.
Daylight doesn't change actors' faces or injury. **[O]** Page dressing, lens and
casting visibility/VO remain editorial; no final paper asset generated here.

## `loc.aurora_exterior` — `ending_lost`

**[C] Function:** intake/exhaust buried, broken test call dies, spring searchers
find four names by ridge door; all four crew dead. Source `ending_lost.arrival`,
screenplay E05. No alive cast in a window or secret second rescue route.

**[A] Space/access:** same low station silhouette, west radio/mast, east annex/
exhaust and north ridge door established in master layout. Snow overlays obscure
it; do not substitute an unrelated station. Spring name insert reuses the ridge
threshold, not a new building. Unnamed searchers can remain offscreen, no extra
actor assets. No playable spring season or simulation.

**[A] Light/weather/shots:** `cam.station_lost` pull-away/dissolve, burial layer
`fx.intake_buried`, spring `fx.spring_light`, `prop.ridge_names` at same door.
Last power glow dies according to lost outcome; even repaired generator cannot
preserve safe shelter after intake loss. Sound ends test-loop battery then wind.
**[O]** Snow burial shape, exterior scale and spring insert need later art/layout
review, not weather gameplay or changed ending rules.
