# `loc.dispatch_hall` — same room, accumulated consequences

## Function / source [C]

Canonical recurring `dispatch`, screenplay D01–D03. The hall connects engineering
and radio, holds Mara/Jonas before a route is chosen, gives heat feedback and
warm-hand opportunity, and later commits crew to the ridge. Sealed or resolved
repair jobs cannot be re-entered as a budget reset. No decision deadline here;
canonical cold accounting still matters when relevant. No new global storm clock.

## Fixed spatial identity [A]

Proposed 6×7m. South: corridor/stores approach; west/left: radio; east/right:
engineering; north/ahead: ridge. Same four threshold centres, duct northwest,
resting bench left of ridge door, wall seams/window bands on every visit.
PLANS holds exact coordinates. A repaired/sealed status may change light and
door dressing, never move a wall or swap left/right routes.

Keep central carry lane free, not filled with new planning furniture. Duct uses
reusable `env.service_duct` (new production proposal), bench
`env.rest_bench` (new proposal); neither adds a new authored prop puzzle.
Actual source props are torch, handset, optional line and carried gear. The ridge
door can remain visually legible before it is offered; looks are not permission
to traverse it early. Door cues must not add another guard or mini-game.

## Cast and return variants [C→A]

Mara supports Jonas at fixed northwest rest mark. Alex chooses routes or warms
hands there. Lea is only physically present if her actual radio responsibility
permits; otherwise a handset/remote line, never another visible Lea in the hall.
Warm light/duct motion reflects `generator_done`; radio-first wait still affects
Jonas. His splint stays with treatment. Hands/heat do not heal the ankle.

Entry heat/radio-first/return cues can repeat whenever eligible: reuse short
acting, don't invent a one-time cinematic state flag. Resolved/lost jobs mark
closed opportunity without erasing successful work. The source ridge choice
opens after both jobs resolved (including explicit abandonments), not after
merely looking through both doorways.

## Light / sound / shots [A]

Blackout: torch/local reserve, cool high-window fill. Warm power: steady practical
key and duct sleeve movement, same palette and bench. Radio success: real reply
heard remotely; manual mode retains Lea's location elsewhere. `cam.hall_routes`
is the reference repeat view, with `cam.line_insert`, a short warming insert
using existing hand/duct intent, and `cam.threshold` for chosen departure.
Cameras stay south initially, engineering reads right and radio left. Return
shots match the first geography before closer reactions. Wind attenuates indoors;
Captions must not imply a second room clock or force a route automatically.
