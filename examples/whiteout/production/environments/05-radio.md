# `loc.radio_cabin` — cabin, wheel and responsibility

## Function / source [C]

Canonical `radio`, screenplay R01–R07. Sixty-second transmitter reserve already
running. Mug/braid jumper or manual alignment can support a real call; automatic
success lets Lea release, manual success needs her at the mast. T=15000 gust
can overlap work while `!radio_auto`. Warm power does not automatically align,
transmit or free her. Cabin access loss cannot invent a call or erase one.

## Connected space / accesses [A]

6×4m cabin, sole hall entrance south. Transmitter desk against northwest wall;
shorted bracket/mug assembly within the same desk station. Mast working bay
immediately beyond north-east opening, in the **same** set/time context. This
maintenance opening is not another selectable route to ridge or an escape.
No corridor, second room, bridge or independent sixty-second timer is added.
Source requires Lea at exterior mast; PLANS makes that relation visible.

Use `prop.radio_transmitter`, `prop.radio_reserve`, `prop.antenna_wheel`,
`prop.radio_jumper`, `prop.radio_door`, `prop.ceramic_mug`, `prop.copper_braid`.
One painted gull/handle mug becomes the installed jumper; never another carried
mug in the next shot. Cable trays route overhead/along wall, not through cast
legs. Sealed high window is weather fill, not an escape aperture.

## Cast / contacts / orientation [A]

From south C0, desk LEFT, mast wheel RIGHT. Alex moves between desk and Lea,
not a cut teleport. Lea's proposed cap/headset, longer blue-grey silhouette,
cream collar and gloved contacts follow Character Bible; empty concept art
contains no redesigned actor. Wheel centre z≈1.1m, radius .32m, Alex/Lea marks
and working threshold in PLANS. These proposed dimensions need final shoulder/
glove rehearsal. No height requirement is added to the game.

Manual success keeps Lea's shoulder/hands at the wheel, including voice lines
heard in the hall. Automatic success releases the same actor's hands. An empty
concept is a layout study, never proof that manual radio runs without her.
Before alignment, a gust catch does not set `dish_aligned`; after adapter
assembly but before auto call, pending gust still follows source predicate.

## Light / weather / camera variants [C→A]

Initial reserve can power receiver/amber readout while station supply is dark;
do not show a warm fully powered ceiling as evidence of generation. With real
`generator_done`, warm cabin practicals/vent are allowed; reserve remains a
separate clock until a successful call cancels it. Exterior snow stays outside
except threshold blow-in. Storm gust uses existing timing, not a weather timer.

`cam.radio_axis` establishes connected stations; `cam.jumper_insert`,
`cam.radio_insert` and medium wheel coverage stay on the south side of the
Alex/control or crew/wheel axis. The gull, insulation gap, hands and timer stay
legible; storm layer must not obscure which person holds duty. A call close-up
contrasts a live reply with opening test loop. `cam.threshold` returns to dispatch
without erasing outside responsibility.

**[O]** Final mast arrangement, safe handwheel/rail geometry, cable articulation,
voice mix and final narrow-space camera fitting remain production rehearsal.
No generic rig or scene-loader system is specified by these needs.
