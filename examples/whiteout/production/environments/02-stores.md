# `loc.emergency_stores` — preparation under the shutter

## Function / source [C]

Canonical `stores`, screenplay S01–S04. Mara braces Jonas; liner and medical kit
are nearest, repair tray contains the single copper braid, gull mug and spanner,
evacuation rack has line/flare/heat pack, terminal has maintenance log. Three
minutes until shutter seals the room. No supplies are granted by a concept image.

## Space / accesses / dressing [A]

Proposed 6×4m with one south shared corridor threshold. The same entry becomes
the departure to dispatch via existing corridor; no back door or unmodeled
escape. Low bench west, tools north-left/centre, log north-east, evacuation rack
east. Use PLANS coordinates; room does not rearrange when an item is taken.
Objects are removed/attached by actual possession effects, not by fading shelves
because a camera changes. Closed background containers are noninteractive,
never an unadvertised second braid or medical solution.

Place `prop.thermal_liner`, `prop.medical_kit`, `prop.splint` preparation near the
bench; `prop.copper_braid`, `prop.ceramic_mug`, `prop.spanner` on low tray;
`prop.safety_line`, `prop.flare`, `prop.heat_pack` at rack;
`prop.maintenance_log` at terminal. `prop.stores_shutter` is overhead at south.
The ceramic handle and gull need close-up readability but no giant logo wall.

## Cast / routes / interaction heights [A]

All four may be present. Jonas stays seated/protected; Mara kneels with ankle
clearance, Alex treats from the opposite working side. Proposed left ankle from
character candidate remains [O] for approval; do not silently mirror it here.
A 2m centre/right lane remains clear to the 2m threshold. Tray about .85m high,
terminal 1.15m, relevant pickup shelf .8–1.4m: actor moves between stations,
not a magical reach across the room. Rehearse exact contacts with final bodies.

The ordinary transfer clears people before shutter animation seals. No new
crushing death or discarded carried gear. Physical geometry and light should
show where choices are, without making every crate selectable.

## Light / weather / cinematography [A]

Reserve indicator, local task lamp and cool sealed-window fill; normal ceiling
supply is out. Shutter mechanism gives direction/pressure through sound without
large dynamic set changes. Damp entry mat, local frost at metal; no blizzard
covering small choice props. `cam.stores_establish` (new proposal) establishes
one threshold, then `cam.stores_tray`, `cam.log_insert`, `cam.injury_insert`,
`cam.threshold` stay on the southern side of the actor/task axis.

## Variants [C→A]

Visible 180000ms deadline begins at entry, seven-second briefing consumes it.
After pickups, the same shelves have appropriate gaps. Treated splint only when
`jonas_treated`; liner overlay only when taken. Log is readable only through its
action/presentation, not an automatic discovery from a close-up.
Timeout or deliberate exit leads to dispatch; inaccessible untaken gear stays.
No re-entry geometry promises another visit. **[O]** Shutter panel timing,
readable prop sizes/GUI safe band and partner carry require practical rehearsal.
