## Shared art direction [A]

Continue the Character Bible's **restrained cinematic naturalism**. Aurora is a
working polar research station, not a spaceship, military base or fantastical
laboratory. Low insulated prefabricated modules, practical exposed service runs,
wide service thresholds, sealed high windows and repaired metal edges establish
an inexpensive, repeatable architecture. Exact building engineering, geographic
orientation, station age and construction manufacturer are not canonical facts.
No copied commercial station, brand or outside concept artist is the reference.

Use rectangular panel bays, shallow ribbing, rounded reinforced door corners,
charcoal lower kick panels and narrow amber reserve indicators as repeat motifs.
Pale grey walls and dull sage-grey frames hold cool light; functional warm work
lamps appear with real power. Cable trays and duct paths explain connections
without turning every wall into a sci-fi control panel. Seams align across shots.

| Material / neutral proposal | Use / continuity |
| --- | --- |
| Wall enamel `#A5ACA8` | Matte insulated panel faces, broad light bounce, restrained cold tint. |
| Door/service steel `#586966` | Dull sage-grey frames and equipment brackets; visibly worn contact edges. |
| Floor/rubber `#343D40` | Quiet low-value ground, snow/dampness collect near thresholds, not glossy black mirror. |
| Unpainted metal `#777E80` | Small latch/wheel/tool highlights, not polished wall skins. |
| Reserve amber `#C18A46` | Small readable instrument markers; not neon decoration or proof of generation. |
| Snow/ice `#CBD5D7` / `#8CA3AA` | Snow diffuse, ice local hard highlight. Don't recolour garments or wash out targets. |

Hexes are **[A] neutral swatches**, not lit-pixel equality or shader parameters.
Keep cast slate/sand, teal, ochre and blue-grey separable from the environment;
no large orange wall behind Jonas or blue-grey wheel blending with Lea's gloves.
These concepts inherit the existing cast identities, not redesign them.

## Wear / moisture / weather [A]

Scuffs at lower frames and handled edges, condensation on cold sealed windows,
windward frost on exposed ribs, damp hems/boot prints near entries. Repeat
specific wear marks in reverse shots; do not regenerate random damage as scene
identity. Snow banks accumulate on horizontal/exterior surfaces. Inside snow
is limited to threshold blow-in and motivated duct material, not a uniform
particle curtain through solid walls. No magical ice that blocks an offered
control or grants new access by melting.

The broader storm and unsafe intake/crossing are **[C]**; particle density,
wind screen direction and accumulation shapes are **[A]** staging. They do not
simulate a global storm clock. WorldState/deadline outcomes govern consequences;
weather layers visualize them. Indoor/outdoor contrasts should remain readable
without inventing heater immunity or a second reserve.

## Light / compositional identity [A]

Blackout has no ordinary powered ceiling illumination: use diegetic torch,
limited battery task light and cool snow/window bounce. Warning red/amber is
small and device-local. Restored power steadies practical work light and warms
faces/duct cues; the storm outside does not become daylight or stop snowing.
Radio's independent reserve can still expire despite warm interior light.

A human-readable cool fill can separate a dark coat from the wall without
adding glowing costume strips. Dark scenes are not an excuse to hide the
single braid, the injury, antenna duty, doors or timer. Neutral concept lighting
and proposed art must remain distinct from actual accepted power state.
Detailed variants and contradictory-combination rules are in STATES.md.

## Source boundary

**[C]** Aurora, corridor/windows/light failure, stores shutter/equipment, dispatch
repair connections, iced annex/access coil, radio reserve/manual mast, broken
wire/ridge markers/intake and ending evidence come from the game.
**[A]** Panel modules, exact dimensions, door positions, palette, assembly kit,
compass axes, lens values and dressing are production choices. **[O]** Final
layout/casting/contacts, real materials and export budgets await subsequent work.
A visible panel or door is not automatically a selectable action. No new plot
room, explorable bunk/lab, escape hatch, permanent ridge safety cable or helipad
is introduced to solve a problem that canon asks the player to solve.
