## Reusable layers and contradictions

Geometry identity is stable beneath dry/damp/frost/burial overlays. Use existing
`fx.light_failure`, `fx.restored_heat`, `fx.mast_snow`, `fx.mast_gust`,
`fx.ridge_drift`, `fx.crossing_erased`, `fx.intake_buried`, `fx.flare_arc`,
`fx.daylight`, `fx.spring_light`. Reusable production layers are listed in ASSETS;
they are proposals, not new canonical counters or a second mutable world.

Do not apply all states at once. A cool unpowered station may have independent
radio/shutter reserve indicators, but not warm normal ceiling supply. A warm
station may still have cold exterior/radio reserve danger. Shutter closure can
coexist with real engine sound, never automatically stops it. A line/mug absent
from carried inventory may be installed/consumed, not a missing-art fallback.
Blackout concept sheets are **empty layout studies**, not literal frames that
remove characters or manual radio duty.

Wind direction is a shared world/layout [O] to lock in rehearsal; screen direction
must transform with camera, not become physically reversed between cuts. Frost
wear masks, marker order and named equipment keep their placement in reverse
shots. Final exposure/light levels require target-device viewing; no artistic
weather image proves an authored deadline or GPU performance.
