## INT. AURORA RESEARCH STATION — NIGHT

**03:17.**

A wide view down the station corridor. Windows frame a wall of moving white;
there is no horizon beyond the glass.

The ceiling lights die one after another, from the laboratory toward the sleeping
quarters. The last strip goes out above ALEX, the field engineer.

For a moment, only the wind.

A torch snaps on. MARA sweeps its beam across the room, counting faces. Alex.
LEA, clutching a hand-held radio. JONAS, on the floor, one leg held painfully
still. The beam catches his crushed ankle and stops.

**MARA**

Four. We're all here.

She lowers the light away from Jonas's eyes and crouches beside him. Alex kneels
on his other side, leaving space around the injured leg. Lea stays close enough
to hear them, her free hand braced against the wall.

A broken distress call comes from Lea's handset.

**CLOSE ON THE RADIO.** Static clips the transmission. A breath is audible between
words. The call repeats: the same break, the same breath, exactly.

Lea looks up at Alex.

**LEA**

Did you send that?

Alex shakes their head. Mara and Jonas give no sign of recognition.

Hold on Lea's listening face. Let the repeated breath register before cutting
away. Do not explain the transmission's origin here.

A metallic groan carries through the station. Alex turns toward engineering.
The generator intake is icing shut. At the antenna controls, the motor indicators
are dark.

**ALEX**

The intake's freezing. The antenna motors have no power.

Mara looks from Jonas to the snow beyond the windows.

**MARA**

Can a helicopter still reach us?

**ALEX**

If we get a signal out before the ridge closes.

Lea tightens her grip on the handset. Another burst of the broken call fills the
silence where an answer should be.

Alex rises. The torch beam follows toward the stores; keep the route visible
without making the other exits look like selectable choices.

**MARA**

Take what you need. Then choose which repair comes first.

Return to a wide composition: Alex standing, Mara beside Jonas, Lea with the
radio. Hold the group together in frame. The player should understand who needs
help, what is failing and why both repairs matter.
